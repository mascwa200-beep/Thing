ships inside the app rather than sitting only in the repository.

Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org

Note on the wider design: this application's LCARS-inspired interface is an
original homage. It bundles no Paramount assets — no screen captures, no
insignia artwork, and no audio from the programmes. Interface sounds are
synthesised at runtime rather than sampled.
