Survival guide diagrams — provenance & licensing
=================================================
The knot illustrations in this folder are scanned engravings from the
1911 Encyclopaedia Britannica (11th edition), which is in the PUBLIC DOMAIN
worldwide (published 1911). Sourced via Wikimedia Commons.

  reef-knot.jpg, clove-hitch.jpg, two-half-hitches.jpg, taut-line-hitch.jpg
  (Midshipman's Hitch), figure-eight.jpg, sheet-bend.jpg, timber-hitch.jpg

No attribution is legally required for public-domain works; provenance is
recorded here as good practice. Only freely-licensed images are bundled.
