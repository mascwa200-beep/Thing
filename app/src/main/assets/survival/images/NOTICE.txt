  phonetics-the-sounds-of-speech-2.png — the source-filter model: a head in profile with the
      vocal folds picked out in one colour and labelled "source", and the pharyngeal, oral and
      nasal cavities above them picked out in another and labelled "filter", with the legend
      spelling out which is which. This is Fant's 1960 model, which the acoustic section states
      in words — the folds produce a harmonic-rich buzz, the tract above resonates and reinforces
